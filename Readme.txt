# Optimization and Scheduling Research Repository

Welcome to my research repository.

This repository contains part of my research works related to:
- optimization,
- scheduling,
- platooning,
- electric vehicles,
- mathematical modeling,
- heuristics, and
- operations research.

Some projects, codes, datasets, and computational results are uploaded gradually as I continue organizing and cleaning the implementation for public release.

The current repository mainly contains:
- selected source codes,
- case studies,
- computational results,
- generated schedules, and
- supporting research materials.


## Research Philosophy

For scheduling problems, if you cannot automatically generate a detailed timetable from your solution, then the feasibility and correctness of the solution cannot be trusted.
